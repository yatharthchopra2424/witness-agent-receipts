Source: https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon and the pasted registration text, 2026-10-21. Dates may change at Meta's discretion.

## Format
Virtual, worldwide, free to enter if selected. Max 20,000 accepted participants.

## Tracks (every project enters one)
- **Track 1, Indie developer (ours):** individual builders and students. Judged on **creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works**.
- Track 2, Startup: teams building a real product; technical execution and production readiness.
Prize pool: **$1,000,000** total across both tracks. Split not published.

## Themes
1. **Build for Your World**: solve a problem you know firsthand.
2. **Push the Frontier**: software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials; give a machine a new capability.
Witness fits Theme 2 (software) and arguably Theme 1.

## How applications are reviewed
Two equally weighted criteria, using **only the application answers**:
1. Project idea: clarity, feasibility, fit to a theme.
2. Technical skill: the AI project you describe, what you built, techniques used. GitHub/LinkedIn/product links help but are optional.
Questions 13–16 are for program improvement and are **not** used in selection.
Eligible applications above cut-offs are accepted automatically (first 20,000); others by score if space allows.

## Eligibility and rules
- Open worldwide except where local law prohibits, and except sanctioned parties/regions (Belarus, China, Russia, Ukraine, Syria, Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions, Cuba, North Korea, Iran). **India is not listed as excluded.**
- Not eligible: employees/contractors/interns of Meta and Cerebral Valley, government-affiliated individuals, their immediate family and household members.
- Duplicate applications, false identity information or spam are disqualified.
- **Official Rules publish before Oct 26 and must be accepted by selected participants.** Read them for: IP ownership, open-source requirements, prior-work rules, how prizes are paid and taxed, data use.
